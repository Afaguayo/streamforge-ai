Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

StreamForge AI ("the app") is a desktop application that runs on your own computer. This policy explains what data it handles and where that data goes.

Summary

  • We don't run servers that receive your data. The app has no account system, analytics or tracking.
  • Everything the app stores stays on your computer.
  • Data only leaves your computer when the app talks directly to a platform you connected (for example, uploading a video you approved to YouTube).

What the app stores on your computer

  • Sign-in tokens and app keys for the platforms you connect, kept in Windows Credential Manager.
  • Account details returned by those platforms, such as your channel or display name and IDs, kept in a local database.
  • Your settings, recording file locations and processing history, kept in the same local database.
  • Videos, clips and thumbnails you create, kept in folders you choose.

Data from platforms (Kick, YouTube, TikTok, Facebook, Instagram)

When you sign in to a platform, the app receives only the permissions you approve on that platform's own consent screen. It uses them only to:

  • show which account is connected (basic profile info such as display name),
  • detect when your stream starts and ends (Kick),
  • upload and publish videos you approved, and
  • read performance statistics for your own posts.

This data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Deleting your data

  • Press Disconnect on an account in the app to delete its tokens. You can also revoke access in that platform's settings.
  • Uninstalling the app and deleting %APPDATA%\com.afaguayo.streamforge removes everything it stored.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
